INSTITUTIONAL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) 2026
CAMPUS AUDITORIUM OPERATIONS & IT INFRASTRUCTURE
Document Reference: SOP-AUD-IT-2026-V2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026 - December 31, 2026
Target Audience: Student Clubs, Event Organizers, Technical Committee Leads, Faculty Mentors
Jurisdiction: Office of Student Affairs, Campus IT Infrastructure, Facilities & Safety Division


1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs all technical workshops, hackathons, seminars, stage productions, and co-curricular operations conducted inside the Main Campus Auditorium, AV control suites, and backstage technical zones. Additionally, it details mandatory procedures for reserving dedicated campus Wi-Fi hotspots and high-density network infrastructure for event participants.


2. PERMISSIONS REQUIRED FOR AUDITORIUM TECHNICAL WORKSHOPS
To host technical workshops, hackathons, or robotics/hardware labs in the Auditorium, organizers MUST obtain all five (5) mandatory clearances listed below before key handover and system activation:

A. Primary Facility Clearance (Dean of Student Affairs - DoSA)
- Form AUD-REQ-01 (Auditorium Facility Booking Application) must be submitted at least 14 business days prior to the event date.
- Requires written endorsement and signatures from the Club Faculty Advisor and authorized Club President.
- Confirms academic calendar compliance and ensures no scheduling conflicts with institutional university events.

B. Technical & Audio-Visual (AV) Division Clearance
- Technical Rider Requisition (Form AUD-TECH-R2) must be submitted to the Chief AV Engineer at least 7 business days ahead.
- Access to stage consoles (Behringer X32/Wing), motorized line-arrays, laser projectors, and DMX lighting grids requires certified operator clearance.
- A mandatory technical soundcheck, walkthrough, and projection test must be scheduled at least 24 hours prior to the event.

C. Electrical Maintenance Division (EMD) Load Clearance
- Mandatory whenever auxiliary equipment draws exceed 3.5 kW (e.g. servers, soldering benches, 3D printer clusters, power supplies, high-intensity moving head lights, or fog systems).
- EMD inspects temporary power distribution units (PDUs), circuit breaker ratings, and cable ramps, issuing a signed 3-Phase Power Safety Seal.

D. Fire & Safety Committee Clearance (Disaster Management Cell)
- Mandatory for any workshops utilizing soldering/heat guns, aerosol sprays, chemical prototyping, aerial drone flight tests, or theatrical atmospheric effects (haze/fog).
- Two dedicated, active CO2 fire extinguishers and safety-trained student marshals must be stationed at the venue throughout the workshop duration.

E. Campus Security & Guest Gate Pass Approvals
- External mentor vehicle entry passes, visitor badges, and external equipment entry clearances must be filed with the Campus Security Office at least 3 business days prior.
- Overnight sessions or setups beyond 8:00 PM require a special Extended Hours Permit countersigned by DoSA and the Chief of Security.


3. CAMPUS WI-FI HOTSPOT & EVENT NETWORK RESERVATION PROCEDURES
To provide dedicated wireless network connectivity for auditorium workshops, coding challenges, or guest delegations, clubs must follow the standard 4-phase reservation workflow:

Phase 1: Online Portal Ticket Submission (Timeline: >= 5 Working Days in Advance)
- Log in to the ClubOps Management Portal -> IT Services -> Request Hotspot & Network Form.
- Enter expected concurrent attendees, total active devices, target auditorium zones (Stage, Hall, Foyer), and estimated bandwidth quota (e.g., 200 Mbps burstable pipe up to 1 Gbps backbone).

Phase 2: Technical Specifications & Firewall Exceptions (Form IT-NET-WIFI-04)
- Technical Whitelisting: If the workshop requires Git operations over SSH (port 22), Docker Hub pulls, cloud APIs, custom TCP/UDP sockets, or live video streaming (RTMP 1935), list ports explicitly in Section 2B.
- The IT Network Security Administrator reviews and signs off within 48 hours of ticket dispatch.

Phase 3: Dedicated SSID Provisioning & Credentials Generation
- IT Network Services provisions an isolated Virtual LAN (VLAN) broadcasting a custom SSID: 'EVENT_<ClubName>_2026' or 'WORKSHOP_GUEST_5G'.
- Organizers receive a secure QR Code and 16-character WPA3-Personal pre-shared key (PSK) or temporary 802.1X enterprise tokens 24 hours prior to workshop start.
- Quality of Service (QoS) guarantees minimum 15 Mbps per attendee device and prioritized 50 Mbps bandwidth for presenter workstations.

Phase 4: Physical Portable Mesh Hotspot / AP Checkout & Return Protocol
- For outdoor workshops or auditorium dead zones, request high-density portable Wi-Fi 6 mesh kits.
- Pickup: Collect portable units from IT Operations Room (IT-104) 1 hour prior by submitting the student ID card of the Club Lead or Tech Coordinator.
- Return: All hotspots, power adapters, and Cat6 patch cables must be returned and inspected within strictly two (2) hours after workshop adjournment.


4. HANDOVER, COMPLIANCE & PENALTY CLAUSES
1. Facility Neutral State: The auditorium stage, lighting faders, seating arrangements, and podium wiring must be restored to baseline condition immediately following the event.
2. Equipment Return Deadline: All portable hotspots, adapters, patch cables, and stage stageboxes must be returned to Room IT-104 within strictly two (2) hours after the event adjournment.
3. Non-Compliance Sanction: Late return or unauthorized electrical modifications results in an immediate 60-day suspension of club privileges from reserving campus auditoriums and IT hotspot facilities, plus hardware replacement fines.
